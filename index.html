Frequently asked

Will treatment hurt?

We use modern techniques and local anaesthesia so you stay comfortable. Tell us if you feel nervous and we will go at your pace.

How often should I visit the dentist?

Most people benefit from a check-up and cleaning every six months, but your dentist will recommend a schedule that suits your needs.

Do you see children?

Yes. We welcome children of all ages and aim to make their first visits short, friendly and stress free.
